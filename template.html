A small macroeconometric model of Norway in the NAM tradition of Bårdsen, Eitrheim, Jansen and Nymoen

ForecastThe system simulated from the last national-accounts quarter under Norges Bank's rate path or a policy rule, with bootstrap bands and the published projections beside it. PolicyA permanent +100 basis points, the loss function of Akram, Bårdsen and Eitrheim (2005) by stochastic simulation, and every coefficient on a slider. EquationsEach estimated equation with its published counterpart, standard errors, diagnostics and recursive tests; the imposed long-run relations against free estimates; the implied equilibrium unemployment rate. RobustnessIndicator saturation, super-exogeneity tests, the New Keynesian Phillips curve against the EqCM, recursive cointegration, OLS against 2SLS. Forecasting recordDynamic simulation over the sample and a rolling pseudo-out-of-sample evaluation from 77 origins against a dVAR, intercept correction, no change and an AR(4). Notes and sourcesDepartures from the published model, the additions beyond it, the sources read and the files in the repository.

Breaks, invariance and encompassing the tests the authors would run next

Five further checks on the estimated equations, in the order a referee from this tradition would ask for them: let the data find the breaks, test whether the equations are invariant to them, confront the inflation equation with the New Keynesian Phillips curve, check that the long-run relations are stable as the sample grows, and check that single-equation OLS has not been misled by a contemporaneous regressor.
